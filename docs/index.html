Did I Feed the Dog?

Support & Help

Getting Started

How do I add a dog?

Tap the + button on the dashboard, or go to Settings → Dogs → Add Dog. Enter your dog's name, birthday, and an optional photo.

How do I log a feeding?

Tap the green Log Meal button on any dog's card. Choose the meal type — Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Snack, Treat, or Custom — and confirm. The card updates immediately.

How do I show my name next to feedings I log?

Go to Settings → Your Name and type your name. It will appear next to every feeding you log so family members can see who fed the dog. If no name is set, the app uses your device model name.

Can I log a meal for all dogs at once?

Yes. When you have 2 or more dogs, a fork icon appears in the top-right of the dashboard. Tap it to open the Feed All Dogs sheet and log the same meal for everyone in one tap.

Can I undo a meal I just logged?

Yes. After logging a meal, a brief Undo banner appears at the bottom of the card. Tap Undo within a few seconds to remove the entry and restore any food portion that was deducted.

Family & iCloud Sync

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes. If everyone in your household is signed into the same iCloud account, feedings, food stock, and dog info stay in sync across all iPhones automatically. Changes usually appear within about a minute.

How do I know which family member fed the dog?

Each feeding in the history shows the name of the person who logged it, next to the time. Make sure everyone sets their name in Settings → Your Name so feedings are clearly attributed.

What if two people log a feeding at the same time?

Both feedings are saved. You may see two entries close together in the history — that's the accurate record of what happened.

Food Stock & Notifications

How do I track food portions?

Go to Settings → Food Stock and choose Per Dog (each dog has their own count) or Shared Pool (one bag for all dogs). Portions decrease automatically when you log a meal. Snack and Treat meals do not reduce the count.

How do I set up feeding reminders?

Go to Settings → Feeding Reminders. Choose All Dogs (one schedule for everyone) or Per Dog (individual schedules). Add up to 3 daily reminder times.

I'm not receiving notifications. What should I check?

Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Did I Feed the Dog? and make sure notifications are allowed. Also confirm the toggles are on in the app's Settings → Notifications.

Siri & Shortcuts

How do I log a feeding with Siri?

Say "Log [dog's name]'s feeding in Did I Feed the Dog?" — Siri will log it instantly, no need to open the app.

How do I check if my dog has been fed?

Say "Did I feed [dog's name] in Did I Feed the Dog?" — Siri will tell you when they were last fed and whether they're overdue.
