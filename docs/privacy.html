Did I Feed the Dog?

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated May 2026

Overview

Did I Feed the Dog? is designed with your privacy in mind. We believe your data belongs to you. The app does not require any account creation, and we do not collect, sell, or share any personal information with third parties. All data you enter stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud account.

Data We Store

The app stores the following data locally on your device and in your private iCloud account:

  • Pet profiles (names, birthdays, and photos)
  • Feeding event records (meal type, timestamps, and the name of the person who logged the meal)
  • Food stock counts and low-stock thresholds
  • Custom feeding schedules and notification preferences

This data is used solely to operate the app. We never access, read, or transmit this data to any external servers operated by us.

iCloud Sync & Family Sharing

If you are signed into iCloud, your data syncs across your devices and with other family members if you have shared access to your app's CloudKit containers. This sync is managed entirely by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Analytics & Tracking

Did I Feed the Dog? does not use any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not track you across other apps or websites. We collect no usage data.

Siri & Shortcuts

When you use Siri or Shortcuts with the app, interactions are processed by Apple on-device or through Apple's infrastructure. We do not receive, store, or transmit any Siri query data.

Notifications

Push notifications (feeding reminders, low stock alerts, birthday alerts, overdue feeding alerts, and water bowl hygiene reminders) are scheduled and delivered locally on your device using Apple's system. No notification content is transmitted to any server operated by us.

Children's Privacy

Did I Feed the Dog? does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts, no sign-up process, and no data collection of any kind.
