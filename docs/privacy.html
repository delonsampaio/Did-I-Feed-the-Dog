Did I Feed the Dog?

Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated April 2025

Overview

Did I Feed the Dog? is designed with your privacy in mind. The app does not collect, sell, or share any personal information with third parties. All data you enter stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud account.

Data We Store

The app stores the following data locally on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud account:

  • Dog names, birthdays, and photos you add
  • Feeding event records (meal type, time, notes, and the name you set in Your Name)
  • Food stock counts and app settings (reminders, thresholds, preferences)

This data is used solely to operate the app. We never access, read, or transmit this data.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, your data syncs across your devices and with other family members on the same iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit service. This sync is managed entirely by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to your iCloud data.

Analytics & Tracking

Did I Feed the Dog? does not use any analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not track you across apps or websites. We collect no usage data.

Siri & Shortcuts

When you use Siri or Shortcuts with the app, interactions are processed by Apple on-device or through Apple's Siri infrastructure. We do not receive or store any Siri query data.

Notifications

Push notifications (feeding reminders, low stock alerts, birthday alerts) are scheduled and delivered locally on your device using Apple's notification system. No notification content is transmitted to any server operated by us.

Children's Privacy

Did I Feed the Dog? does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts, no sign-up process, and no data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at delon@sampaio.dev.
